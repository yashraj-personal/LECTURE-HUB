POLICY

What this portal collects, why, and how it's used.

OVERVIEW

A private, invite-only archive

Lecture Hub is a private learning portal. It is not indexed by search engines and is intended only for people who have been given access. This page explains, in plain terms, what limited information the portal keeps track of.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Visit & activity data

To keep the archive working and to understand how it's used, the portal records:

• A randomly generated visitor ID, stored in your browser's session storage — it isn't tied to your name or email.
• Basic visit and page-view events (timestamp, page, and browser user agent).
• Actions like opening a lecture link, viewing notes, or using the search/filter tools.
• Whether a portal or admin password entry succeeded or failed (the password itself is never stored in logs).

WHAT WE DON'T COLLECT

No tracking beyond the portal

This site does not use advertising cookies, does not sell any data, and does not share visit data with third parties. No payment or card details are ever collected by the portal — any support/donation payment happens entirely inside your own UPI app.

WHERE IT'S STORED

Google Sheets backend

Activity logs and visit counts are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the portal administrator. External lecture, notes, and quiz links open on their own third-party sites, which have their own separate privacy practices.

QUESTIONS

Get in touch

For any question about this policy or your data, reach out via the Contact page.
